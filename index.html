ABOUT THE CLINIC

Care, guidance and awareness.

Trust Pharma Clinic is based in Gopalganj, Bihar. The clinic's focus includes patient education, medication review, health counselling and follow-up care. We aim to make medicine-related information easier to understand and encourage safe, informed conversations with qualified healthcare professionals.
